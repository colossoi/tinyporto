Fiat 500 (1970) - Simplified Exterior v3

27,394 triangles. Simplified exterior with opaque windows and four wheels.
Both rear vent areas are smooth panels with embedded color/shadow textures.
The headlights have round lenses, circular chrome rims and simple reflectors.
Four shallow inner fender wells use the existing body paint and close the
openings behind the wheels, with clearance around the tires.
Cabin details, the floor and undercarriage components have been removed.

Based on "Fiat 500 - 1970 Model" by Parsa Farvadian:
https://sketchfab.com/parsafarvadian

Original model:
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/fiat-500-1970-model-213cd126fcd744809fb7996eb88366f5

and undercarriage, made cabin windows opaque, replaced geometric vents with
textures, rebuilt round headlights, and added painted wheel-well liners. Original modeling credit: Parsa Farvadian.
